Band guidance, applied to each category as a proportion of its maximum:
  90-100%  Evidence directly and repeatedly demonstrates the requirement, at the
           depth and seniority the JD asks for.
  70-89%   Evidence clearly demonstrates the requirement, with a minor gap in
           depth, recency or scale.
  50-69%   Partially relevant evidence, or an adjacent/transferable equivalent
           the context supports.
  25-49%   Weak or indirect evidence only (for example a bare skills-list
           mention with nothing behind it).
  0-24%    No supporting evidence in the resume.

Additional rules:
  - Years of experience: compare only against a minimum the JD actually states.
    If the JD states none, do not penalise the candidate on duration.
  - If the resume does not state total experience and it cannot be derived from
    employment dates written in the resume, record that under
    missing_information and score on the demonstrated evidence you do have.
  - Certifications count towards Education only where the JD asks for them or
    they are clearly relevant to the role.
  - Preferred requirements can never make up for a missed mandatory requirement.
